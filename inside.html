to whoever opens this laptop

An Open LetterAn Open Letter

To whoever opens this laptop,

I never liked websites that feel like templates. The ones I remember feel made by hand: someone chose every line, every pause, every colour.

So that is how I work. I start with a pencil, not a framework. I sketch until the idea is clear, then I build it in 3D and in code until it moves the way it moved in my head.

I care about the details nobody asks for: how quickly the first screen shows up on a cheap phone, how a page feels when you scroll it slowly, how the light falls across a desk at five in the afternoon.

Every project here was built for someone real, and every one taught me something the last one couldn't.

If you have an idea that deserves to feel real, I would love to draw it with you.

Mohamed Samy
